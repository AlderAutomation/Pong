## Rules
- first to 3 wins 
    - need to keep two counters for play scores
- space bar to serve the ball
- ball gets served in random angle from center 
- ball has to bounce on upper and lower horizantal window bounds 
- ball has to destroy on going through vertical window bounds
    - also counter for other player has to go up 
    - and ball has to reserve
